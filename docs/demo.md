# A 90-Second HisabGuard Demo

Use synthetic TRIP-104. Describe the operator as illustrative unless a real interview has been completed.

| Time | Show | Explain |
|------|------|---------|
| 0-10s | Trip exception queue | Three trip packets; the owner needs an exception investigator, not another ledger. |
| 10-25s | TRIP-104 customer and driver accounts | BDT 32,000 invoice, BDT 25,000 received; BDT 7,000 outstanding. The BDT 1,500 advance balance is separate. Open an actual synthetic original from an evidence ticket. |
| 25-45s | Investigate trip; persisted tools and question | With live credentials, show the real tool calls. Without credentials, explicitly call this a deterministic review demonstration. |
| 45-60s | Record driver response: "Customer handed me BDT 7000 in cash. I still have it. No receipt or handover record." | Reinvestigate. Money remains outstanding — the driver statement is not owner-confirmed receipt evidence. The investigator asks the driver for cash handover documentation, not the customer again. |
| 60-75s | Reassessed question and evidence citations | Show the narrowed question citing specific evidence IDs and the advance balance context. |
| 75-90s | Follow-up, evidence citations, owner approval | Approve the request for a payment receipt, then download the approved draft. Nothing has been sent or paid. Closure remains blocked on unpaid money. |

For a complete closure walkthrough, add a draft payment record for BDT 7,000, review it against its source, then record and review the BDT 1,500 cash return. Investigate again and explicitly approve settlement closure. Typed records are operator attestations, not bank verification.

Other scenarios: TRIP-106 lacks delivery proof; TRIP-108 repeats a fuel transaction id. The investigator requests different evidence, and the duplicate is counted at most once. Excluding a duplicate retains its original and audit trail.

- Synthetic data and images, not actual clients or transactions.
- No bKash API, WhatsApp sending, GPS tracking, money movement, fraud verdict or independent signature verification.
- Live model and speech accuracy are unmeasured until the team supplies credentials and tests genuine inputs.
- Model tools cannot approve evidence, approve follow-ups, change ledger amounts or close settlements.
